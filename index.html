About Me

I am a political science researcher based in Tijuana, Mexico. My work focuses on understanding political dynamics in the border region and beyond.

My research interests include comparative politics, public policy, and political institutions. I am committed to rigorous empirical research that contributes to our understanding of governance and political behavior.
